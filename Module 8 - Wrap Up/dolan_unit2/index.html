Sunshine Web Services is a “walk-in” web tune-up service for businesses, creators, and developers. The goal is simple: make your site feel faster, look cleaner, and communicate its message clearly on phones and desktops. Instead of rebuilding everything from scratch, we focus on high-impact fixes that noticeably improve the user experience in as little as a weekend. Whether you run a small shop, portfolio, or personal brand we can help it perform better without starting over.

Our checkups are designed around real issues most sites have in common: oversized images, messy spacing, confusing navigation, missing alt text, and pages that don’t pass basic validation. By pairing a quick audit with targeted, prioritized changes, we help you ship a more professional site without losing your existing branding or content. No full rebuilds, no surprises, and no confusing technical jargon. Just clear, measurable improvements you can see and feel right away.

Our Tune-Up Menu is built for quick wins that make a real difference. Speed improvements include compressing and properly sizing images, reducing unnecessary CSS and JavaScript, and improving layout stability so content doesn’t shift while loading. Accessibility improvements include adding descriptive alt text, improving color contrast ratios, and ensuring the entire site can be navigated using only a keyboard. These changes make your site easier and more welcoming for every visitor.

SEO and clarity upgrades focus on structure: meaningful headings, better page titles, readable sections, and consistent spacing throughout. We also help clean up common HTML and CSS validation issues so, your codebase is easier to maintain going forward. The result is a site that feels more credible, trustworthy, and professional. One that communicates its purpose and value clearly within the first few seconds of a visitor landing on the page. Small structural fixes often have the biggest impact on how visitors perceive your brand.

Every checkup starts with a short intake: what the site is for, who it's for, and what "better" looks like to you. From there, we run a structured audit that evaluates layout consistency, responsiveness across screen sizes, and common performance problems like oversized images and unoptimized web fonts. This step also reviews document structure. Headings, landmark sections, and the overall reading flow to identify what's working and what isn't.

After the audit, we apply fixes in priority order and then re-verify the site against the original findings. We don't aim for perfection; we aim for noticeable, meaningful improvement. When the work is done, you receive a short-written report describing exactly what changed, what could still be improved later, and a few optional "next upgrade" suggestions. This keeps the entire engagement realistic, transparent, and easy to hand off to a teammate or continue improving on your own schedule. Most clients find that even a single focused session gives them a much clearer picture of where their site stands and what to prioritize next.

Mandy’s Bakery: A local shop site looked fine on desktop but completely collapsed on mobile screens. Navigation overflowed, text was unreadable, and buttons were too small to tap. We simplified the layout using a single-column grid, tightened spacing, and resized interactive elements to meet touch target guidelines. The result was a dramatically more usable mobile experience with no changes to the desktop design.

South Florida Trading Cards: A small business site was using uncompressed, full-resolution images that ballooned page weight and slowed load times significantly. We resized, compressed, and converted images to modern formats, cutting load time in half while keeping the visual quality intact.

Bob’s Towing: Their site had weak structural foundations. Headings were skipped or misused, landmark sections were missing, and the page felt unfinished. We built the content flow with proper heading hierarchy, consistent typography, and descriptive alt text on every image. These small but targeted improvements have created a noticeable jump in professionalism and overall credibility.

To book a checkup, start by choosing your primary goal: speed, accessibility, SEO, or overall polish. Then send a link to your live site, or a ZIP folder if your project is still in development. Include any notes about pain points, things you've already tried, or areas you're most concerned about. The more context you provide upfront, the faster we can get to work.

Response time is typically within one business day for scheduling confirmation. After that, you'll receive a short intake checklist covering any additional assets we may need, such as your logo, brand colors, key content notes, and any required before-and-after screenshots. Even if you're not ready for a full site refresh, a focused one-session checkup can make your project look noticeably cleaner and feel more complete. Businesses, freelancers, artists, and independent creators all benefit from a fresh set of eyes. Sometimes a small fix in the right place is all it takes to turn a visitor into a customer.
