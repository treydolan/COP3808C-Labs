Sunshine Web Services

Speed. Accessibility. SEO. Handled by us, so you can relax.

Sunshine Web Services is a fictional “walk-in” web tune-up service for small businesses, creators, and student projects. The goal is simple: make your site feel faster, look cleaner, and communicate its message clearly on phones and desktops. Instead of rebuilding everything, we focus on high-impact fixes that improve the experience in a weekend.

Our checkups are designed around real issues most sites have: oversized images, messy spacing, confusing navigation, missing alt text, and pages that don’t pass basic validation. By pairing a quick audit with targeted changes, we help you ship a more professional site—without losing your existing branding or content.

Our Tune-Up Menu is built for quick wins. Speed improvements include compressing images, reducing unnecessary CSS/JS, and improving layout stability so content doesn’t jump while loading. Accessibility improvements include adding alt text, improving color contrast, and ensuring the site can be navigated by keyboard. These changes make your site easier for everyone to use.

SEO and clarity upgrades focus on structure: meaningful headings, better page titles, readable sections, and consistent spacing. We also help clean up common validation issues so your HTML and CSS are easier to maintain. The result is a site that feels more trustworthy and easier to understand within a few seconds of landing on the page.

Every checkup starts with a short intake: what the site is for, who it’s for, and what “better” looks like to you. Next, we run a quick audit that looks at layout consistency, responsiveness, and common performance problems like large images and unoptimized fonts. This step also checks structure—headings, sections, and the overall reading flow.

After the audit, we apply fixes in priority order and then re-check the site. We don’t aim for perfection; we aim for noticeable improvement. You get a short report describing what changed, what to improve later, and a few “next upgrades” that are optional. This keeps the work realistic and easy to maintain.

Case Study A: A portfolio page looked fine on desktop but collapsed on mobile. We simplified the layout, improved spacing, and made the buttons easier to tap. Case Study B: A small business site used huge images that slowed everything down. We resized and compressed images, reducing load time while keeping the design intact.

Case Study C: A student project had weak structure—headings were skipped, sections were unclear, and the page didn’t feel “finished.” We rebuilt the content flow with consistent headings, improved typography, and added accessible alt text. Small improvements created a big jump in professionalism.

To book a checkup, choose a goal: speed, accessibility, SEO, or overall polish. Include a link to your site (or a ZIP folder if it’s local), plus any notes about what you want to improve. If you’re a student, you can also include your rubric requirements so the work targets exactly what you need to submit.

Response time is typically within one business day for scheduling. You’ll receive a short checklist of what to send (logo, colors, content notes, and any required screenshots). Even if you’re not ready for a full refresh, a focused checkup can quickly make your project look cleaner and feel more complete.
